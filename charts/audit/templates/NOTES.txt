Check what a profile keeps:
  kubectl exec deploy/{{ include "audit.fullname" . }} -- audit profile explain <name> --deployment /etc/audit/deployment.yaml

Verify the chain yourself, with nothing but the archive and the public key:
  audit verify --profile <name> --from <date> --to <date> --bucket {{ .Values.bucket }} --public-key <file>
